FILAMENT

Rules

Setup. Play on a patch of the Cairo pentagonal tiling where every cell has either 3 or 5 neighbors. Two cells are neighbors only if they share an edge; cells that touch only at a corner are not neighbors. The board starts empty, and Black moves first.

Place. On your turn place a stone of your color on an empty cell.

Fill. After a placement, determine if any neighboring empty cells have a majority where more than half of all its neighbors hold stones of one color: at least 3 of 5 for inner cells, or at least 2 of 3 for edge cells. Fill each such cell with a stone of the opposite color of the majority. Fills can create new majorities. Continue filling until none remain. Then your turn ends.

Game end. The game ends when the board is full. The player with fewer groups wins.

Groups. Two of your stones are in the same group if you can trace a path between them that steps only from neighbor to neighbor through your own stones. A stone with no neighbors of its color is a group by itself.

Ties. If you tie on group count, each player lists their group sizes from largest to smallest. Compare the two lists one position at a time: first the largest groups, then the second-largest, and so on. At the first position where the sizes differ, the player with the smaller group wins. If every size matches, the game is a draw.
