Players alternate placing a stone of their color on an empty cell. If a local majority (3/5 or 2/3) is created, then fill that cell with the opposite color and repeat until no such local majorities remain on the board. When the board is full, the player with the most groups wins; ties go to the player with the largest single group, and any remaining tie goes to the player who placed the final stone. Set P1 (Black, who always moves first) and P2 independently to Human or AI above; the AI uses UCT Monte Carlo tree search with an adjustable thinking time.
